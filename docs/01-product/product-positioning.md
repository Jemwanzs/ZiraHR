# Product Positioning

## What ZiraHR is

A cloud HRMIS/SaaS covering the full employee lifecycle:

**Recruit → Hire → Onboard → Manage → Pay → Develop → Perform → Engage → Offboard**

## What ZiraHR is not

Not "another HR system." Not a feature-list product. Not sold through long paragraphs and stock photography.

## Positioning statement

> One platform. Every stage of your people journey.

## Supporting message

> From recruitment and onboarding to payroll, leave, attendance, performance, learning and offboarding — ZiraHR gives your teams one flexible place to run HR.

## Alternative hero copy (for A/B testing, not simultaneous use)

- "Everything your people team needs. Finally, in one place."
- "Your people. Your processes. One intelligent HR platform."

## Brand adjectives

Modern. Calm. African. Intelligent. Premium. Human. Alive.

## The core proof mechanism

ZiraHR's differentiator isn't a claim, it's a demonstration: **one employee record** underlies every module (Payroll, Leave, Attendance, Performance, Learning, Documents, Assets, Recruitment, Lifecycle, Disciplinary). The website's job is to make that architecture *visible* — via the "One Employee, One Journey" scroll story and the "Everything Connects" section — rather than assert it in copy.

## Voice

Calm, professional, contemporary, slightly playful, confident. Never exaggerated. No unsupported statutory/automation/security claims ("bank-level security" etc.) unless substantiated by a real, demonstrable product capability.

## Funnel this website is built for

**Discover → Understand → Trust → Experience → Convert** — mirrored directly in the homepage section sequence (see `homepage-scope.md`) and in the three-tier CTA system (`Start with ZiraHR` / `Request a Demo` / `Login`).
