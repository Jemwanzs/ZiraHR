# ZiraHR Website & Digital Experience — Source Scope

> Transcribed verbatim (structure preserved) from the supplied PDF `ZiraHR Website & Digital Experience.pdf`. This is the canonical source scope for the marketing website. Do not edit this file to "fix" or reinterpret requirements — capture reinterpretations in the other `/docs` files instead, and note deviations explicitly where they occur.

**Product:** ZiraHR
**Positioning:** Cloud HRMIS / SaaS
**Primary Market:** Africa, starting with Kenya
**Core Goal:** Discover → Understand → Trust → Experience → Convert

## 1. Primary Objective

Build a premium, high-performance, SEO-first product website that presents ZiraHR as a complete employee lifecycle and workforce management platform.

The website must communicate within seconds that ZiraHR brings the employee journey together:

**Recruit → Hire → Onboard → Manage → Pay → Develop → Perform → Engage → Offboard**

Do not build a conventional HR software website filled with long paragraphs and generic stock photographs. The website should **show the actual product working** — real ZiraHR screens, cropped product UI, short product videos, animated workflows, interactive cards, dashboards and tasteful motion.

The experience should feel: **Modern. Calm. African. Intelligent. Premium. Human. Alive.**

## 2. Core Positioning

ZiraHR should not sell itself as "another HR system." Position it around:

> **One platform. Every stage of your people journey.**

Supporting message:

> From recruitment and onboarding to payroll, leave, attendance, performance, learning and offboarding — ZiraHR gives your teams one flexible place to run HR.

Alternative hero copy to A/B test:

> Everything your people team needs. Finally, in one place.
> Your people. Your processes. One intelligent HR platform.

Keep copy extremely concise. No giant blocks of marketing text.

## 3. Website Information Architecture

Primary navigation: **Platform | Solutions | Payroll | Resources | Pricing | Company**
Right navigation: **Login | Request Demo | Start Free / Sign Up**
Sticky navigation bar with subtle glass/blur behaviour after scrolling.

**Intelligence** — Dashboards, Analytics, Reports, Ask TiJa AI

## 4. Homepage — Section by Section

**01 — Hero.** Eyebrow: "THE HR PLATFORM BUILT AROUND YOUR PEOPLE". Headline: "One place to run your entire people operation." Supporting: "Recruit. Onboard. Manage. Pay. Develop. Perform. ZiraHR connects your entire employee journey." CTAs: "Start with ZiraHR →", "Request a Demo", tertiary "Already using ZiraHR? Log in". Hero visual: not a static dashboard screenshot — a cinematic product composition with an Executive Dashboard at the center and Employee Profile, Leave approval, Payroll, Attendance, Performance, Recruitment, Ask TiJa assembling around it as the visitor scrolls. Concept: many HR processes → one ZiraHR workspace. Use genuine product UI.

## 5. Product Story — "One Employee. One Journey."

Scroll-driven employee lifecycle story. "Meet Amina." Walk through: Applicant → Candidate → Offer → Employee → Onboarding → Attendance + Leave → Payroll → Learning → Performance → Promotion → Offboarding. Show tiny pieces of the actual ZiraHR interface handling each stage — purpose is to prove these aren't disconnected modules; they share one employee record. Signature experience of the site.

## 6. "Everything Connects" Section

Headline: "One employee record. Everything connected." Beautiful employee profile in the middle, animated connection lines to: Payroll, Leave, Attendance, Performance, Learning, Documents, Assets, Recruitment, Lifecycle, Disciplinary. Visitor should visually understand ZiraHR's architecture without reading an explanation.

## 7. Module Showcase

Not twelve identical feature cards — an interactive showcase: left = module navigation, right = large product demonstration.
- **Core HR** — "Your people records. Finally organized." Show: employee profile, job details, employee directory, lifecycle, organization structure, documents.
- **Payroll** — "From gross to net. Without the chaos." Animated workflow: Draft → Review → Approve → Process → Disburse. Include payroll run, statutory calculations, payslip, reports, bank file/disbursement.
- **Leave** — "Leave that practically manages itself." Request → Workflow → Approval → Balance Updated.
- Continue similarly for Attendance, Performance, Recruitment, L&D, Collaboration.

## 8. Payroll Feature Page (`/payroll`)

SEO targets: payroll software Kenya, Kenya payroll software, HR and payroll software, cloud payroll system Kenya. Hero: "Payroll without spreadsheet gymnastics." Showcase: Payroll Cycles, Basic Pay, Allowances, Benefits, Deductions, PAYE, NSSF, SHIF, Housing Levy, Reliefs, Salary Advances, Payslips, Payroll Approvals, Bank Files, Disbursements, Payroll Analytics. Use real UI extensively. Do not make unsupported statutory or automation claims merely for marketing.

## 9. Leave Management Page (`/leave-management`)

Hero: "Leave management that keeps everyone in sync." Demonstrate: Employee requests leave → Manager receives approval → Workflow routes automatically → HR sees calendar → Balance updates. Show: leave policies, accruals, carry forwards, multi-level approvals, leave calendar, balances, reports, ESS.

## 10. Attendance Page (`/attendance-management`)

Hero: "Know who's working. Without chasing attendance sheets." Showcase: Clock In/Out, Geo-fencing, Attendance rules, Branch locations, Late arrivals, Overtime, Shifts, Rosters, Timesheets, Attendance analytics. Include mobile UI mockups.

## 11. Core HR Page (`/core-hr`)

Show: Employee Directory, Employee 360 Profile, Organization Structure, Branches & Regions, Departments, Job Grades, Job Positions, Employee Lifecycle, Promotions, Transfers, Temporary Assignments, Onboarding, Offboarding, Documents. Primary message: "One reliable record for every employee."

## 12. Recruitment Page (`/recruitment`)

Visually demonstrate: Requisition → Job Board → Applications → Shortlist → Interview → Offer → Hire. Final animation transforms the candidate into an employee profile — visually proves ZiraHR is one connected platform.

## 13. Performance Page (`/performance-management`)

Show: Goals, KPIs, Review Cycles, Appraisals, Continuous Feedback, 360 Feedback, PIP, Analytics. Headline: "Turn goals into conversations. Conversations into growth."

## 14. Learning & Development (`/learning-development`)

Show: Skills → Learning → Assessment → Certification → Growth. Feature: Training Programs, Courses, Enrollments, Assessments, Certificates, Skills, Competencies, Development Plans.

## 15. Teams & Collaboration

Show ZiraHR as more than an administrative system: Workspaces, Teams, Channels, Messaging, Announcements, Notifications, Employee collaboration. Keep energetic and visually interactive.

## 16. ESS — Give Employees Their Own Experience

Major homepage section. Headline: "HR isn't only for HR." Then: "Give every employee a simpler way to manage their work life." Show desktop + mobile together. Employee can: View Profile, Update Details, Download Payslips, Request Leave, Check Leave Balance, View Attendance, View Goals, Complete Learning, Receive Notifications, Access Documents, Follow Approvals.

## 17. Flexibility — A Major Differentiator

Headline: "Your organization isn't generic. Your HR software shouldn't be either." Show actual Settings UI. Animate configurations: Regions → Branches → Departments → Roles → Workflows → Leave Policies → Payroll Rules → Security. Message: "Configurable without becoming complicated."

## 18. Approval Workflow Experience

Animated workflow builder: Employee → Supervisor → Department Head → HR → Finance → Approved ✓. Switch the same visualization between Leave, Payroll, Salary Advance, Promotion, Disciplinary, Data Imports. Message: "One approval engine. Your rules."

## 19. Ask TiJa AI

Dedicated cinematic section, dark background may temporarily interrupt the cream/white site. Headline: "Meet TiJa. Ask your workforce." Animated example questions: "Who has contracts expiring this month?", "Show pending leave approvals.", "Explain this month's payroll variance.", "Which employees have missing bank details?" Show TiJa responding through the real interface. Position as "Workforce intelligence inside ZiraHR" — not a generic chatbot.

## 20. Dashboards & Analytics

Show four dashboard experiences: Executive, HR, Finance, My Dashboard. Animated horizontal gallery, hover/tap brings a dashboard forward. Headline: "Different roles. Different questions. The right answers."

## 21. Multi-Region / Multi-Branch

Important for larger African organizations. Show: Organization ↳ Region ↳ Branch ↳ Department ↳ Employee. Demonstrate data visibility: Regional Manager sees "My Region", Branch Manager sees "My Branch", Executive sees "Whole Organization". Headline: "One organization. However complex."

## 22. Security & Governance

Avoid generic "bank-level security" claims unless substantiated. Demonstrate real capabilities: Roles & Permissions, Data Visibility, MFA, Audit Logs, Approval Workflows, Export Controls, Working-Hour Controls, Geo-fencing, Activity Logs. Headline: "Control who sees what. And know what happened."

## 23. Customer Proof

Eventually populate with actual customers and verified evidence: "Trusted by growing teams" + logos, then 2–4 high-quality testimonials, preferring Problem → ZiraHR → measurable result over generic praise. **Never manufacture testimonials, customers, employee counts or savings.**

## 24. Interactive Product Tour

"Explore ZiraHR" — let visitors interact with a lightweight simulated interface without logging in. Buttons: Run Payroll, Request Leave, Approve Request, Open Employee Profile, Ask TiJa, View Dashboard.

## 25. Video Strategy

Several short clips, not one giant corporate video: Hero Film (20–30s, product montage), Core HR (10–15s, employee profile interaction), Payroll (15–20s, payroll lifecycle), Leave (10s, request → approval), Attendance (10s, clock-in → dashboard), TiJa (15s, question → answer). Autoplay muted only where performance permits, pause off-screen, static poster fallbacks.

## 26. Visual Design System

- **Primary Canvas — Warm Cream** ≈ `#FFF9F2` (extremely subtle warmth, not yellow-cream)
- **White** `#FFFFFF` — used generously
- **ZiraHR Sky Blue** — interactive elements, links, data, selected states, product highlights
- **Deep Teal / Blue** — sparingly: primary CTAs, navigation emphasis, strong product sections
- **Warm Orange** — brand energy color: small accents, highlights, new badges, motion, secondary CTA states
- **Soft Gray** — background separation and typography hierarchy

Avoid filling the website with blue. The product UI itself should provide much of the colour.

## 27. Typography

**Outfit** as primary brand family (if aligned with ZiraHR). Headlines: Bold/Semi-Bold. Body: Regular. Use huge display typography selectively ("HR. Connected." / "People operations, without the chaos."). Avoid excessive uppercase.

## 28. Motion Language

Borrow Apple's philosophy, not Apple's website — motion communicates hierarchy and relationships, not decoration. Use: scroll reveals, product screen stacking, mask reveals, subtle parallax, counter animations, animated connectors, morphing cards, horizontal product galleries, sticky storytelling sections, soft hover elevation, subtle magnetic CTA effects, cursor-reactive product cards (desktop only). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 29. CTA System

Three primary conversion paths: **Primary** "Start with ZiraHR", **Sales** "Request a Demo", **Existing Customer** "Login". Hover: arrow travels right, background transitions smoothly, button raises 1–2px, no aggressive bouncing. Sticky CTA may appear after visitors cross the hero.

## 30. Request Demo Page (`/request-demo`)

Keep extremely simple. Capture: First Name, Last Name, Work Email, Phone, Company, Country, Employee Count, Modules Interested In, Preferred Contact Method. CTA: "Book My ZiraHR Demo". After submission: Thank you → Confirmation → Calendar/meeting scheduling where implemented.

## 31. Sign-Up Experience (`/signup`)

Progressive onboarding, not one huge form. Step 01 "Let's meet you." Step 02 "Tell us about your organization." Step 03 "How is your team structured?" Step 04 "Welcome to ZiraHR." Then redirect into the existing ZiraHR organization setup journey.

## 32. Resources / SEO Content Engine

Resources: HR Guides, Payroll Guides, Compliance, Templates, Product Updates, HR Glossary, Help Centre, Blog. Should not become an SEO article dumping ground — genuinely useful HR resources only.

## 33. SEO Architecture

Dedicated pages around actual search intent rather than trying to rank the homepage for everything: `/hr-software-kenya`, `/payroll-software-kenya`, `/leave-management-software`, `/attendance-management-system`, `/performance-management-software`, `/recruitment-software`, `/employee-self-service`, `/hrmis-kenya`, `/hr-software-africa`. Then country-specific payroll/product pages where ZiraHR actually supports those countries. Each page needs unique copy — never duplicated pages with the country name swapped.

## 34. Technical SEO

Semantic HTML, clean heading hierarchy, canonical URLs, XML sitemap, robots.txt, breadcrumbs, OpenGraph, X/Twitter metadata, Product/Organization/SoftwareApplication/Breadcrumb structured data, FAQ structured data only where genuinely applicable, descriptive image alt text, internal linking, human-readable URLs, redirect management, proper 404, server-rendered/indexable content, search-friendly module pages.

## 35. Performance Is Part of the Design

Do not sacrifice Core Web Vitals for an Apple-like appearance — hitching animations destroy the premium effect. Requirements: responsive images, AVIF/WebP, lazy-loaded below-fold assets, optimized video, poster images, code splitting, minimal third-party scripts, font preloading, no giant autoplay video on mobile, lazy animation initialization, reduced-motion alternative. Target: **Lighthouse 90+ across Performance, Accessibility, SEO and Best Practices** on production-equivalent builds.

## 36. Mobile Experience

Design dedicated mobile compositions, not shrunk desktop. Shorter hero, swipeable product screens, sticky bottom CTA where appropriate, mega menu becomes a categorized drawer, lighter video clips/posters, touch-friendly horizontal galleries, natural typography scaling, simplified interactive demos.

## 39. The Most Important Instruction

> Do not build the entire site as a long feature list. Build a product story. Use the existing ZiraHR application as the source of truth for product capabilities, terminology, navigation, screenshots and workflows. Before implementing each marketing section, inspect the corresponding working module in the ZiraHR codebase and represent the actual product experience. Do not invent functionality merely to improve marketing copy.

> The visitor should understand ZiraHR visually before they finish reading the text. Prioritize genuine product UI, interactive demonstrations, short copy, strong hierarchy, whitespace, motion and storytelling.

**Deviation note:** the real ZiraHR HRMIS codebase is not present in this repository/environment. Until real screenshots/video are supplied, sections that call for genuine product UI are built with clearly-labeled placeholder mockups (see `/docs/08-assets/screenshot-plan.md`), swapped for the real thing as assets arrive.

## 40. Final Homepage Journey

01 This is ZiraHR → 02 See it (cinematic product reveal) → 03 Follow an employee (Recruit→Onboard→Manage→Pay→Grow→Exit) → 04 Explore the platform (module showcase) → 05 See employees use it (ESS) → 06 See how flexible it is (settings/workflows/org structure) → 07 Meet TiJa → 08 Understand your organization (dashboards/analytics) → 09 Trust it (security/permissions/auditability) → 10 Believe it (customers/testimonials) → 11 Experience it (interactive product tour) → 12 Convert ("Ready to run HR differently?" — Start with ZiraHR → / Request a Demo).

> It doesn't merely tell prospects that ZiraHR has Payroll, Leave, Attendance and Performance. It lets them experience how the pieces become one platform before they ever create an account.
